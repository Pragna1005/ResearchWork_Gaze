# Sources and licences of the reading texts

All texts are used unmodified. One typo in a practice answer option ("2n" → "2nd") was corrected.

## OneStop paragraphs and questions
OneStopQA: Berzak, Y., Malmaud, J., Levy, R. (2020). STARC: Structured Annotations for Reading
Comprehension. ACL. https://github.com/berzak/onestop-qa (annotations/onestop_qa.zip).
Licence: CC BY-SA 4.0. Texts are Guardian articles from the OneStopEnglish corpus
(Vajjala & Lučić, 2018), in Advanced (original) and Elementary (simplified) versions.

Articles used (first 4 paragraphs each): 101-Year-Old Bottle Message; A New Form of Lie Detector
Test; Bright Future for Astrotourism; Google Introduces its Driverless Car; Inky the Octopus
Escapes from Aquarium; Insects Could be the Planet's Next Food Source; Japan Calls Time on Long
Hours Work Culture; Why is Sweden Closing its Prisons?
Practice: Vienna Named World's Top City for Quality of Life (Intermediate, paragraphs 1–2).
